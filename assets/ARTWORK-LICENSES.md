- **Record:** https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/786759

## durga-java.jpg

- **Work:** Durga Mahishasuramardini; Central Javanese Period, 9th century.
- **Medium:** Stone (andesite).
- **Collection:** Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art, Freer Gallery of Art, F1914.54.
- **Credit:** Gift of Charles Lang Freer.
- **Record:** https://asia.si.edu/object/F1914.54/
- **Image source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Durga_Mahishasuramardini_(%E2%80%9CSlayer_of_the_Buffalo_Demon%E2%80%9D).jpg

- **File:** Wikimedia’s 960-pixel thumbnail of the collection photograph.

Both images were downloaded on October 12, 2026. Their historical context is explained alongside the images; neither is presented as a literal illustration of a specific Kavacham verse.

## Navadurga photographs

Downloaded on October 13, 2026. Each card includes the file record, author, and
license link. Full URLs, dimensions, download sources, and credits are recorded
in [navadurga/media.json](navadurga/media.json).

Eight photographs show Navadurga sculptures at Sanghasri, Kalighat, Kolkata,
in 2010. The author credited by Wikimedia Commons is **Jonoikobangali**.
