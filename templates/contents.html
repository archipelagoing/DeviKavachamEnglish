A guide to the prayer

Find your place in the Kavacham.

The hymn moves from naming the Goddess to asking for protection in every direction, throughout the body, and across daily life.

These themes are a reading guide to this 56-verse edition. They are editorial groupings, rather than chapter titles in the original hymn.

  1. 01–02

    The question & the armor

    Mārkaṇḍeya asks Brahmā about protection; Brahmā introduces the holy armor of the Goddess.

    ↗
  2. 03–05

    The nine forms of Durgā

    The Navadurgā are named, from Śailaputrī to Siddhidātrī.

    ↗
  3. 06–08

    Refuge in times of fear

    The hymn describes taking refuge in the Goddess amid danger and remembering her with devotion.

    ↗
  4. 09–16

    The Mothers & their powers

    Forms of the Goddess, their vehicles and weapons, and an invocation of her strength.

    ↗
  5. 17–20

    Protection in every direction

    Guardians of the compass directions, above and below, before and behind.

    ↗
  6. 21–35

    The body as sacred ground

    Protection moves through the head, voice, limbs, organs, and joints. Verse 21 bridges the directions and the body.

    ↗
  7. 36–39

    Breath, senses & inner life

    Mind, intellect, the five vital breaths, sense experience, the guṇas, dharma, and knowledge.

    ↗
  8. 40–42

    Family, journeys & what remains

    The prayer extends to family, livelihood, paths, and every place left unguarded.

    ↗
  9. 43–45

    Moving through life with courage

    The hymn describes wearing this armor wherever one goes, and celebrates fearlessness and victory.

    ↗
  10. 46–53

    Recitation & its devotional fruits

    Faith, daily recitation, and the hymn’s promises of protection. Verse 53 connects the Kavacham to the Caṇḍī Saptaśatī.

    ↗
  11. 54–56

    Continuity, grace & liberation

    The closing verses turn toward descendants, the grace of Mahāmāyā, and the supreme state beyond bodily life.

    ↗
